## Objective

Sector and department containers use the sheet's styling instead of
placeholder yellow and red, and a colour key explains what the colours mean.

## Background

`OperationsSectorLayout` (`bg-yellow-300`) and `DepartmentLayout`
(`bg-red-100`) are rounded cards with placeholder fills. In the sheet they are
header bands, and a KEY explains the role colours. Without a key, a new HR
member has no way to know that yellow means "lead".

## Files you will touch

- `src/components/SpreadsheetLayout.tsx` (`OperationsSectorLayout`,
  `DepartmentLayout`)
- `src/components/ColorKey.tsx` (new)
- `src/pages/members.tsx` (render the key once)

## What to change

1. Restyle the sector and department headers to match the audit (fills, type,
   borders). Drop rounded corners and padding that the sheet does not have,
   unless your audit argues for keeping them.
2. Build `ColorKey`: a compact legend that maps each role/status colour to its
   meaning. Drive it from the **same token list** as the cells, so adding a
   token cannot leave the key out of date. A small array of
   `{ token, label }` in one place, imported by both the cells and the key, is
   enough.
3. Put the key where the sheet puts it (top of the page, if that is where the
   sheet has it), visible without scrolling past every team.
4. Remove the stray `console.log(sector.name)` in the members page render loop
   while you are rendering the key there.

## How to approach it

- Do W45.6.2 or this one first, whichever you prefer. They touch different
  components in the same file, so one PR each keeps reviews small.
- The key is information, not decoration. Make it a real list (`<ul>`/`<dl>`)
  so a screen reader reads "Pale yellow: Lead", not just colours.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No `bg-yellow-300`, `bg-red-100` or `bg-purple-*` placeholders remain in
      `SpreadsheetLayout.tsx`.
- [ ] Sector and department headers match the audit.
- [ ] The colour key lists every role/status colour used in the cells, from a
      single shared source.
- [ ] The key is semantic HTML and readable by a screen reader.
- [ ] `npm run build` passes, and the PR includes a screenshot next to the
      sheet.
