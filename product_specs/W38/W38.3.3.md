## Objective

Close the loop: drop a dragged member on another team and have the spreadsheet
actually change. This is the spec where the three parallel chains meet.

## Background

You are assembling three finished pieces:

- **W38.3.2** gives you a drag that carries a member identifier.
- **W38.3.1** gives you `moveMemberToTeam(members, email, fromPosition, destination)`.
- **W38.1.3** gives you the member list in React state and a way to update it.

Your job is the wiring, plus one genuinely non-obvious browser behaviour:

> **An element does not accept a drop unless its `dragover` handler calls
> `event.preventDefault()`.**

The default action of `dragover` is "reject this drop". If you skip the
`preventDefault`, your `drop` handler simply never fires and there is no error
message anywhere. This is the single most common cause of "my drag and drop
doesn't work". Budget for it.

The second thing to watch: drop targets nest. A team sits inside a department
inside a sector. If all three accept drops, one drop can fire three handlers as
the event bubbles. Decide where the drop belongs and stop the event there.

## Files you will touch

- `src/components/SpreadsheetLayout.tsx`
- `src/pages/members.tsx` (only if the updater plumbing needs adjusting)

## What to change

1. On `TeamLayout`'s root, handle `onDragOver`, `onDragLeave` and `onDrop`.
   `onDragOver` must `preventDefault()` for the drop to be possible, and should
   set `event.dataTransfer.dropEffect = "move"`.

2. Highlight the team under the cursor — a ring or a background shift. Clear it
   on `dragleave` and on `drop`.

   You will find `dragleave` fires when the cursor crosses onto a *child* of the
   team, making the highlight flicker. Solve it. The usual approaches are a
   counter that increments on `dragenter` and decrements on `dragleave`, or
   checking `event.currentTarget.contains(event.relatedTarget)`. Pick one and
   comment why.

3. In `onDrop`:
   - Read and parse the payload from the custom MIME type. **Wrap the
     `JSON.parse` in a try/catch** — a user can drag a file or a selection of
     text from another window onto your target and you will get something that is
     not your JSON.
   - Ignore drops whose payload is not one of ours.
   - Resolve the payload to the actual member and the actual source `Position`
     from current state.
   - Build the `destination` object from the team this `TeamLayout` is rendering
     — its team name, and the department / cluster / sector it sits under. You
     may need to pass those down as props from `DepartmentLayout` /
     `OperationsSectorLayout`; a team does not currently know its own parents.
   - Call `canMoveToTeam`. If it says no, stop — do not mutate. (Surfacing the
     reason to the user is W38.3.4; a `console.warn` is fine for now.)
   - Copy the member array, call `moveMemberToTeam` on the copy, and push the
     copy into state. Re-read the warning in W38.1.3 about reference equality
     before you write this — this is exactly where it bites.
   - Set the new position's `supervisor` to the destination team's lead, since
     W38.3.1 deliberately left it undefined. Handle a team with no lead.
   - `stopPropagation()` so ancestors do not also handle it.

4. Confirm the tree rebuilds: the member disappears from the source team and
   appears in the destination team without a page refresh.

## How to approach it

- **Do the smallest possible version first:** `onDragOver` with
  `preventDefault`, and an `onDrop` that only does
  `console.log("dropped", event.dataTransfer.getData(...))`. Do not write another
  line until that log appears. Everything after it is ordinary React.
- Then add state update. Then highlighting. Then supervisor resolution.
- If the member moves in the data but the screen does not change, you handed
  React the same array reference. If the screen changes but the member appears in
  both teams, your destination fields are incomplete and `buildOrganization` is
  grouping them somewhere unexpected — log the new `Position` and look at it.
- Pair on this one if you can. Four dependencies converge here and two sets of
  eyes on the payload round-trip saves an hour.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Dragging a member from one team and dropping on another moves them, with no
      page refresh.
- [ ] The member is gone from the source team and present in the destination team.
- [ ] The member's other current positions are unaffected.
- [ ] `positionHistory` grew by one; the old position now has an `endDate`.
- [ ] The hovered team highlights, and the highlight does not flicker when the
      cursor passes over members inside it.
- [ ] Dropping a member on their own team does nothing (no duplicate position).
- [ ] Dragging arbitrary text or a file from outside the app onto a team does not
      throw and does not change any data.
- [ ] One drop produces one move, not one per nested container.
- [ ] Refreshing the page resets to the original data (expected in Phase 1).
- [ ] `npm run build` passes.
- [ ] Your PR includes a screen recording of a successful move.
