## Objective

Make the card appear when you hover a member in the spreadsheet, and disappear
when you stop.

## Background

`TeamLayout` in `src/components/SpreadsheetLayout.tsx` maps over `team.members`
and renders a column of `<span>`s per member. That column is the hover target.

The hard part is not showing the card — it is showing it *well*. Three problems
you will hit, in the order you will hit them:

1. **Instant appearance feels twitchy.** Moving the mouse across a row of twelve
   members should not flash twelve cards. A short delay before showing fixes it.
2. **The card disappears when you move toward it.** If the card is a sibling of
   the member cell and there is a 4px gap between them, the mouse crosses dead
   space and `onMouseLeave` fires. A short delay before *hiding* fixes it.
3. **The card gets clipped.** `TeamLayout`'s root has `overflow-hidden`, and the
   whole spreadsheet is a stack of nested flex containers. A card that is a
   normal child of that tree will be cut off at the team's edge. Getting it to
   escape is the real work of this spec.

## Files you will touch

- `src/components/SpreadsheetLayout.tsx`

## What to change

1. Track which member is hovered. One piece of state in `TeamLayout` holding the
   hovered member (or `null`) is enough — do not add a boolean per member.

2. Attach `onMouseEnter` / `onMouseLeave` to each member's column and render
   `<MemberHoverCard>` for the hovered one, passing the `currentPosition` that
   `TeamLayout` already computes for that member.

3. Add the two delays. Roughly 150–250ms before showing, and 100–200ms before
   hiding. Implement them with `setTimeout`, and **clear the pending timer** when
   the opposite event fires or the component unmounts — otherwise you get a card
   that pops up after you have already left, or a React warning about setting
   state on an unmounted component.

4. Solve the clipping. Two approaches, both acceptable:
   - **Portal** — `createPortal` the card to `document.body` and position it with
     `position: fixed` using coordinates from the hovered element's
     `getBoundingClientRect()`. Escapes every `overflow-hidden` ancestor. More
     code, fully reliable.
   - **Absolute positioning** — `position: absolute` on the card inside a
     `position: relative` member cell, plus a high `z-index`, plus removing or
     working around the `overflow-hidden` on `TeamLayout`'s root.

   Pick one, get it working, and write a comment saying which you chose and what
   went wrong with the other if you tried both. If you go the absolute route and
   find yourself fighting a third ancestor's overflow, switch to the portal —
   that is the signal.

5. The card must not block the mouse. If it renders under the cursor and
   swallows events, you get a flicker loop: card appears → covers cell →
   `mouseleave` → card hides → `mouseenter` → repeat. Offset the card from the
   cursor, and/or set `pointer-events: none` on it (note this makes the card
   itself un-hoverable — fine for now, W38.2.4 revisits it).

## How to approach it

- Get it working with **no delays and no positioning** first: hover, card appears
  somewhere, ugly but present. Then add delays. Then fix positioning. Three
  small working states beat one big broken one.
- If you see the flicker loop, it is almost always problem 5. Check whether the
  card is under the cursor before changing anything else.
- `useRef` is the right place to keep a timer id — putting it in `useState`
  causes a re-render every time you set it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hovering a member in any team shows that member's card; moving away hides
      it.
- [ ] The card is not clipped by the team, department or sector containers, at
      the top-left team and the bottom-right team of the page.
- [ ] Sweeping the mouse quickly across a whole team does not leave multiple
      cards on screen and does not leave one stuck open.
- [ ] No flicker loop.
- [ ] Every `setTimeout` has a matching clear, including on unmount.
- [ ] No React warnings in the console during hover.
- [ ] A comment records which positioning approach you chose and why.
- [ ] `npm run build` passes.
