## Objective

Make the hover card usable by someone who is not using a mouse, and stop it
running off the edge of the screen.

## Background

A hover-only interaction is invisible to anyone navigating by keyboard, and the
HR Committee will be using this tool for hours at a time on a laptop. "It works
if you have a mouse and a big monitor" is not done.

This is also where we deal with the cases W38.2.3 was allowed to ignore: the
member in the rightmost team whose card would render off-screen, and the member
in the bottom row whose card would render below the fold.

## Files you will touch

- `src/components/SpreadsheetLayout.tsx`
- `src/components/MemberHoverCard.tsx`

## What to change

1. **Keyboard access.** The member cell should be focusable and should show the
   card on focus, hide it on blur. That means:
   - giving the cell a `tabIndex`,
   - handling `onFocus` / `onBlur` alongside the existing mouse handlers,
   - making `Escape` dismiss the card while it is open.

   A `<div tabIndex={0}>` gets you focus but tells a screen reader nothing.
   Look up `aria-describedby` and decide whether it applies here, and write a
   sentence in your PR on what you concluded. Getting this partially right and
   understanding why is the goal — not shipping perfect ARIA.

2. **Viewport-edge flipping.** When there is not enough room to the right, the
   card renders to the left. Not enough room below, it renders above. Compare the
   hovered element's `getBoundingClientRect()` against `window.innerWidth` /
   `innerHeight` and choose a side. Two `if`s, not a positioning library — do not
   add a dependency for this.

3. **Card stays open when you hover the card itself.** If W38.2.3 used
   `pointer-events: none`, reconsider it now: users will want to select an email
   out of the card. Making it hoverable means the show/hide logic has to treat
   "cursor is over the trigger" and "cursor is over the card" as one region. The
   hide-delay from W38.2.3 is what makes the gap between them survivable.

4. **Empty and degenerate states.** Verify against real `data.ts` members:
   - a member whose entire `positionHistory` is one `{ role: "M", startDate }`,
   - a member with no `discord` and `github: "NONE"`,
   - a member with eight or more past positions — the timeline must scroll or
     wrap inside the card's max height, not grow past the viewport.

5. **Motion.** Add a short fade or scale on appearance if you like, but respect
   `prefers-reduced-motion`. Tailwind has a `motion-reduce:` variant. One line.

## How to approach it

- Test keyboard navigation for real: click the address bar, then press Tab
  repeatedly into the page. If you cannot reach a member cell, `tabIndex` is not
  doing what you think.
- Test edge flipping by scrolling to the rightmost team and the bottom-most team
  at a narrow browser width, not by trusting the maths.
- Chrome DevTools → Rendering → "Emulate CSS prefers-reduced-motion" checks item 5
  in five seconds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tabbing to a member cell shows its card; tabbing away hides it.
- [ ] `Escape` closes an open card.
- [ ] Near the right edge of the viewport the card flips left; near the bottom it
      flips up. Verified at 1280px and at 768px width.
- [ ] Text inside the card can be selected with the mouse without the card
      closing.
- [ ] A member with 8+ past positions produces a card that fits on screen.
- [ ] Any appearance animation is suppressed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- [ ] Your PR states what you concluded about ARIA for this pattern.
- [ ] `npm run build` passes.

## Out of scope

- Touch devices. The HR Committee works on laptops; mobile is a later problem.
- Editing anything from the card.
- Any change to the model layer.
